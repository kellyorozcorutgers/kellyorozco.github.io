01 / COMMUNITY SERVICE

Puentes Pa’l Futuro

Through the Latino Student Council, I participate in Puentes Pa’l Futuro, a community service program that connects Rutgers students with children at Drift Preschool in downtown New Brunswick. We visit the school for events and activities, building positive connections with the children and supporting a welcoming learning environment.

The partnership began in Fall 2025 after Drift Preschool reached out to the council. With 98% of its families identifying as Latino, the school saw an opportunity for our students to serve as encouraging role models and strengthen connections between the university and the local community.

Community engagement · Teamwork · Bilingual communication
02 / COMMUNITY RESEARCH

YPAR: Language Accessibility in School Admissions

I led a Youth Participatory Action Research project examining challenges faced by ESL students during the high school application process. I translated a school application into Spanish, conducted surveys, and presented our findings at Rutgers University and multiple high schools.

The project helped make the application process more accessible to students and families who speak Spanish.

Research · Spanish translation · Public speaking
